# Video script

Narration lines that must be said on camera. The rest of the script is written around them.

## Chapter 6: Funds committed

> The $63,000 goes into an account of its own for this PO. Its rules let money go to exactly two places: Haoyun, or back to Astro. Thirty percent goes when the motors leave Shenzhen, the rest for what actually arrives.
>
> To be clear about what this is: it does not lock the money the way a real escrow would. It is a commitment. The rules are enforced by the Agent API, and Astro, as the owner, could change them. For a hackathon demo, a visible commitment is the honest version.
>
> And this runs on testnet at 1 to 1,000. The faucet gives 100 test dollars an hour, so the screen shows $63,000 and the account really holds $63.

## Chapter 7: Shenzhen to the Chicago dock

> The bill of lading and the export clearance both came in, so the $18,900 advance goes to Haoyun on Base Sepolia. At the dock, two signals have to agree: customs cleared and the attested count, 44 of 50. Haoyun gets $36,540 for what arrived. $7,560 stays with Astro for the 6 missing.

## Chapter 8: Paid

> First, a payment to an address that is not Haoyun's. The account refuses it, live. That refusal is in the drawer.
>
> With a prepaid wire, that $7,560 would already be gone. Here it never left Astro's control, and at the deadline it goes back.
>
> Everything here runs on Tidepool, a community testnet of the NEAR Intents Agent API. It is not run by NEAR. Moving to mainnet is a change of one address.
